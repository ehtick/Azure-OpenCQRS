# Thank you

Your Memoria Web licence is active, and the tool may read as many services as your edition covers
from now.

Two emails are on their way: a receipt and tax invoice from Paddle, the merchant of record for the
sale, and a licence certificate naming the licensee, the edition, the service count it covers and
the term dates. Keep the certificate for your own records. If either has not arrived within a few
minutes, check the spam folder before [getting in touch](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucabriguglia).

There is nothing to install, activate or unlock — Memoria Web is the same build whichever edition
you run it under. So the next step is to install the archives for the stores you want to read:

- [Memoria Web](tools/memoria-web.md) — what each page shows
- [Configuration](tools/memoria-web-configuration.md) — the manifest, the connection strings, the roles
- [Deployment](tools/memoria-web-deployment.md) — building it and putting it somewhere

Your Settings page lists the services each installed archive declares, which is where you check how
many of them your edition is covering.

Support comes with your edition: the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/lucabriguglia/Memoria/issues)
for Community and Standard, email for Professional and Enterprise — reply to the certificate email
and it reaches the right place.
