# Thank you

Your licence is active, and you may use Memoria in closed-source software from now.

Two emails are on their way: a receipt and tax invoice from Paddle, the merchant of record for the
sale, and a licence certificate naming the licensee, the edition, the developer count it covers and
the term dates. Keep the certificate for your own records. If either has not arrived within a few
minutes, check the spam folder before [getting in touch](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucabriguglia).

There is nothing to install, activate or unlock — Memoria is the same package on NuGet whichever
licence you use it under. So the next step is the same as it ever was:

- [Install](getting-started/install.html) the packages you need
- [Quickstart: mediator](getting-started/quickstart-mediator.html), or
  [Quickstart: event sourcing](getting-started/quickstart-event-sourcing.html)
- [Guides](guides/) for a specific task, [Reference](reference/) for the configuration surface

Support comes with your edition: the [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/lucabriguglia/Memoria/issues)
for Community and Standard, email for Professional and Enterprise — reply to the certificate email
and it reaches the right place.
